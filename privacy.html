Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
